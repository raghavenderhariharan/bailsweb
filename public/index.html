The Ground

Built for cricketers.
Framed by the hills.

Tucked away in Dayara, Rampally, on the Keesara side of Hyderabad, Bails is a turf ground with a pavilion facing a wide, sprinkler-fed outfield and a ridge of rocky hills along the horizon. The mornings are misty, the outfield is green and the wickets play true.

Players say the green wickets offer good early bounce for the quicks, then slow up and reward the spinners later in the innings. The groundstaff keep conditions consistent from the first ball of the day to the last.

  • Turf centre wickets with sight screens at both ends
  • Pavilion with dugouts, a visitors' lounge and a scorer's tower
  • Hosts HCA division league matches, the Bails Cricket League, corporate tournaments and weekend T20 and 50-over cricket

Amenities

Everything for a full day's cricket

From the first net to the presentation ceremony, it's all here at the ground.

Turf wickets

Well-prepared green tops: bounce for seamers early, turn for spinners later.

Lush outfield

A smooth, quick, safe outfield that's carefully maintained for fielding.

Sprinkler irrigation

Rain-gun sprinklers keep the outfield green right through the season.

Pavilion & dugouts

Spacious covered dugouts and a pavilion that looks straight down the ground.

Visitors' lounge

Shaded seating so friends and family can relax and cheer their team.

Canteen & cafeteria

Fresh snacks, tea and meals that regulars say taste home-cooked.

Clean washrooms

Separate, hygienic washrooms for men and women.

Drinking water

Safe drinking water for players and spectators all day.

Ample parking

Plenty of on-site car and bike parking for teams and visitors.

Wheelchair access

Wheelchair-accessible car park and entrance.

Sight screens

Proper sight screens for clear visibility at both ends.

Scorer's tower

An elevated scoring box with a view of the whole ground.

Umpires & scorers

Match officials and scorers can be arranged with your booking.

Match balls

Match balls available at the ground.

Tournament hosting

Experienced with leagues, corporate events, trophies and ceremonies.
